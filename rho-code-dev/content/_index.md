Meet **rho**

Your AI pair programmer. Not a chat bot that generates snippets — a collaborative partner that reads your code, runs your tests, fixes your errors, and works through problems with you in real time.

<section class="feature-section">

## **Works Alongside You**

rho doesn't just respond to prompts — it reads your files, runs your tests, and iterates with you. Describe what you want to build and rho handles the mechanics while you stay in control of the architecture.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **Understands Your Project**

Every edit is informed by your full codebase. rho navigates your project structure, understands your dependencies, and makes changes that fit — not just syntactically, but contextually.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **Rust-First, Language-Flexible**

Deep integration with `cargo`, compiler diagnostics, and the Rust ecosystem. But the same collaborative workflow works with any language you throw at it.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **Runs on Your Machine**

Your code never leaves your computer. rho runs entirely locally — no cloud, no telemetry, no privacy trade-offs. Just you and your pair programmer.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **Extensible with TypeScript**

rho's toolset isn't fixed. Write TypeScript extensions using Deno to add custom capabilities — new tools, integrations, or workflows. The extension system is built right in, so you can tailor rho to your exact needs.

</section>

<section class="feature-section">

## **You Control the Cost**

rho supports OpenRouter out of the box, giving you access to hundreds of models at competitive rates. Or bring your own API key for any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — your choice, your budget, no lock-in.

</section>

---

### **How It Works**

- **Describe what you want** — Tell rho what you're building, and it starts working
- **Watch it work** — rho reads your files, runs `cargo check`, and applies fixes
- **Review and refine** — You stay in the loop, guiding the direction and architecture
- **Ship with confidence** — Tests pass, errors are fixed, and the code fits your codebase
rho is headless by design — it runs as a standalone agent, not tied to any interface. Choose the UI that fits your workflow:

- **rho-ui** — A cross-platform GUI built with [Makepad](https://makepad.nl), providing a native desktop experience
- **rho-code** — A TUI founded in TypeScript, for terminal-first developers

All UIs connect to the same rho engine, so you can switch freely.
